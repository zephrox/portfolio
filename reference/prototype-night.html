It started at a science fair

Before I trained a single model, I helped found the Barguna Science Society and organized its science fair. That was where I first learned that an idea only counts once someone else understands it.

Later, at AIUB, the same habit earned me a poster presentation award from the Department of Physics.

Then I wanted things to move

Read the paper

At AIUB I started building robots: a soccer bot for the intra-university competition, and an entry at the Technoxian World Robotics Championship in 2024.

In 2025 I built DisasterX, an ESP32-CAM rover that streams video and sensor readings from places too dangerous to send people. It became my first paper as first author, published alongside a seismic sensor network we designed for earthquake prediction.

Robots kept misreading faces

A robot that responds to people has to read their faces, and raw classifiers flicker with every blink. For CogniLink, my desktop companion robot, I tracked 468 facial landmarks and wrote Sticky Focus, a filter that waits for 85% agreement before the robot changes its mood. Behavioral jitter dropped by 90%.

That problem became my thesis: facial expression recognition that holds up when pose and lighting change, using anatomical disentanglement and contrastive learning. Along the way I co-authored work on reading emotion from EEG signals.

See CogniLink

Then I turned to my own language

Dense Bangla text can shut out readers with dyslexia. Shohoj Lipi, built for the SciBlitz AI Challenge 2026 at IEEE CUET, scores how hard a passage is, flags difficult sentences with a fine-tuned BanglaBERT model (85.1% validation accuracy) and rewrites them with an LLM while keeping the facts intact.

It reads aloud, marks syllable boundaries and shows exactly what changed, so the reader can always check the original.

Try Shohoj Lipi

Research only matters once it ships

Today I work as a full-stack developer. I built the website for Greensoft Solutions, an IT and cybersecurity firm that works with the Supreme Court of Bangladesh and the ICT Division, and Chakrify, which reads a CV and scores it against a job description.

Shipping taught me what papers don't: load times, failure cases and the person on the other side of the screen.
